ARCADE FOOTBALL / PRIVACY

PRIVACY
POLICY.

Effective October 4, 2026 · Drab Studios

Drab Studios provides the Android game Arcade Football. This policy explains the information handled by the game and this website. Questions or requests can be sent to Cow2kie@gmail.com.

Game information stored on your device

The game saves your settings and progress on your device, including preferences, match and season results, career progress, records, and achievements. It does not require an account. The current game does not provide an online leaderboard or cloud save service, and Drab Studios does not receive these local save files through normal gameplay.

Advertising and consent

Arcade Football includes the Google Mobile Ads (AdMob) SDK to show an interstitial ad at a halftime break. During development, ads may be shown in test mode; released builds may show live ads. In either mode, Google's ads and consent software may process data as described below.

Google's ads SDK may collect and share your IP address (which can indicate a general location), app interactions such as launches and ad views, diagnostic and performance information, and device or account identifiers such as the Android advertising ID when available. Google uses this information for advertising, measurement, and fraud prevention. The exact ads and data use can depend on your device settings and consent choices.

The game uses Google's User Messaging Platform to request and manage advertising consent where required. When available, you can revisit those choices in the game under Settings → Data → Privacy Options. You can also manage or reset your Android advertising ID in your device settings.

Google processes ads and consent information under its own policies: Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from apps and sites.

Sharing and security

Ad and consent information is sent to Google through its SDKs for the purposes described above. Local game progress stays in the app's device storage unless you share your device or use an operating-system backup feature. Google states that data sent by its Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit. We do not operate a separate game account or cloud save server.

Retention and deletion

Local progress remains on your device until you delete it. The game's Settings → Data screen lets you reset season, career, and progress data. To remove all local app data, including saved settings, use your device's clear-app-data control or uninstall the game; your operating system may retain a backup under its own settings. Google controls retention of information processed by its ads services; see its privacy policy and account controls for details.

If you email us, we use your address and message to respond and keep the correspondence only as needed for support or legal purposes. You may request deletion of correspondence by emailing Cow2kie@gmail.com.

Children and families

The game does not ask players to create an account or enter personal information to play. If you are a parent or guardian and have a question about a child's data, contact us at the address above. Advertising data practices are described in the Advertising and consent section.

This website

This static website does not include our own analytics or advertising scripts. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, whose operator may process technical information when you visit. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the game or its data practices change. The effective date above will change when we publish an update. For privacy questions, access requests, or deletion requests, email Cow2kie@gmail.com.
